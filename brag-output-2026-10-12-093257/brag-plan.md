# Brag Plan: IOE Admission Hub

## What is this app?
IOE Admission Hub is a mobile-first admission toolkit for Nepal's Institute of Engineering applicants: estimate admission probability, inspect constituent-campus seats, and build a practical preference sequence from historical data.

## The angle
Turn admission uncertainty into a calm, data-led decision flow. A candidate enters one merit rank, sees the historical probability for a real target, then zooms out to the 2,040-seat campus network and finishes with a Reach / Target / Safety sequence.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Open on a sharp vertical close-up of the real product identity: `IOE Admission Hub`, the IOE emblem, and the line `Your rank. Your options.` The rank field types `240` as the interface wakes up.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The real Rank Predictor is configured for Pulchowk Campus, Civil Engineering, Regular, Open Merit, then resolves to a historical cutoff and a readable probability tier.
- The real Campus Explorer expands to the constituent network and lands on `Total 2,040 Seats`, with campus pills and the seat matrix visible.
- The real Priority Form Sequence Optimizer turns rank `1450` into three colored bands: Ambitious, Realistic, and Safety.

## Outro / punchline
End on the IOE Admission Hub lockup with `Make the list before the list makes you.` Keep the independent-tool disclaimer small and readable beneath it.

## User flow worth showing
Enter merit rank -> choose campus/program/category -> read historical probability -> explore seats -> generate a balanced priority sequence.

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: quiet exam-season command center, with precise data reveals and a premium blue signal
- Interpretation: dramatic camera movement and decisive cuts, but restrained copy and enough hold time for every UI label to read.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

- Background: `#f8fafc` with white surfaces

IOE Admission Hub turns one entrance rank into a clearer admission plan: probability checks, 2,040 constituent seats, and a Reach / Target / Safety priority sequence in one place.

- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`
- Music treatment: fade in from 0.0s, sit around 0.28-0.34 volume, duck slightly under the final lockup, fade at 20.0s
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset `assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json`; strong cues at 8.74s, 13.11s, and 17.47s; beat grid for small UI arrivals at 6.56, 7.09, 7.64, 8.19, 8.74, 11.46, 12.02, 12.55, 13.11, 13.64, 14.20, 14.73, 15.29, and 15.84s.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; let the main blue glow and result card presence breathe with RMS/bass energy, never a waveform or equalizer.
- SFX posture: sparse and polished; a soft UI click for the rank action, a muted impact for the result, and one restrained final logo hit.
- Audio-coupled moments: typed rank, result reveal, seat-count landing, three priority bands appearing in sequence, final lockup.
- Restraint rule: audio must support confidence and clarity, never make the data feel like a game or imply guaranteed admission.

## Storyboard

Sequential/interaction: yes — simulate typing the rank, then a cursor/tap on the predictor action.

### Scene 2 — The signal — 3.5s
The real predictor controls resolve: Pulchowk Campus, Civil Engineering, Regular, Open Merit. The historical cutoff and probability tier arrive as the visual focal point.
Sequential/interaction: yes — controls settle in order, then the result card lands as one unit.
Audio intent: a single soft impact at the result reveal.
Audio-coupled idea: result landing near the 3.27s / 3.82s local beat neighborhood, prioritizing readability over exact sync.

### Scene 3 — The network — 5.0s
The view pulls back into the real Campus Explorer. Campus pills sweep from Pulchowk toward WRC and ERC; the seat matrix appears, then `2,040 Total Seats` becomes the clear read.
Sequential/interaction: yes — campus tabs and table rows reveal in sequence; keep the full seat count held for at least 1.2s.
Audio intent: widening, confident, informational.
Audio-coupled idea: seat-count reveal beat-locked near 8.74s; subtle bass response in the blue accent.
Transition mood: cinematic crossfade -> Scene 4

### Scene 4 — The sequence — 4.0s
The real Priority Form Sequence Optimizer receives rank `1450`. Three bands arrive one by one: `Ambitious / Reach`, `Realistic / Target`, `Safety Backups`.
Sequential/interaction: yes — rank input, generate action, then three readable tier rows; reveal text every other beat and hold the full set.
Audio intent: precise momentum, three light arrivals rather than a dense montage.
Audio-coupled idea: beat-grid around 13.64, 14.73, and 15.84s; accent the first and final tier only.
Transition mood: soft crossfade -> Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Decide clearly — 5.0s

Sequential/interaction: none.

Audio-coupled idea: final lockup may land near 17.47s; keep the final copy visible through 20.0s.
Music: cinematic bed fading under the lockup.
Transition mood: hold -> end
